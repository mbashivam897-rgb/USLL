## 1. Investment View (why HOLD)

United Spirits is the highest-quality way to own India's spirits-premiumisation theme, and the market knows it. The `Company & Market Insights` sheet tells the story cleanly: **Prestige & Above (P&A) volumes have compounded from 33m cases (FY14) to 53m (FY25)** while the company **deliberately exited mass "Popular" (87.7m → 10.9m cases)**. Total volume fell, but value and margin rose sharply — a textbook premiumisation/mix-up. The portfolio (McDowell's No.1, Royal Challenge, Signature, Black Dog, Godawan luxury malt, plus Diageo's Johnnie Walker, Don Julio, Tanqueray, Smirnoff, Captain Morgan) anchors the premium and luxury tiers.

That quality is, however, **already in the price**. At ₹1,247 the stock trades on **34.5x FY27E EV/EBITDA and ~49x FY27E P/E** — rich in absolute terms, even if at a slight discount to the peer median on EV/EBITDA. Our blended fair value of **₹1,240** sits on top of the current price: the modest re-rating case on EV/EBITDA is offset by an intrinsic DCF that flags limited valuation support. We rate the stock **HOLD** and would **accumulate on dips toward ₹1,100–1,150**.

## 2. Which Multiples Are Relevant (and which we discard)

A real spirits valuation does **not** average four multiples. We keep only what is decision-useful and discard the rest with reasons:

| Multiple | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **EV/EBITDA** | **KEEP — primary** | The industry-standard metric for spirits and the **only multiple on which USL and its peers are genuinely comparable** (capital-structure- and D&A-neutral). USL's 37x and peers' 32–41x sit in the same range — no heroic re-rating required. |
| **P/E** | **KEEP — cross-check only, USL-normalised** | Useful as the metric equity investors quote, **but peer P/E is not a valid comparable here**: United Breweries is a *beer* company and Radico/Allied Blenders are higher-growth, lower-base names, so they trade at 65–86x vs USL's ~52x. We therefore apply **USL's own normalised P/E (52x ≈ its 3-yr average and the peer lower-quartile)**, not the peer mean of 72x. |
| **EV/EBIT** | **DISCARD** | For an asset-light model (capex ~1% of NSV, `Assumptions`), EV/EBIT ≈ EV/EBITDA, so it adds no information and double-counts. It is also distorted upward by United Breweries' ~58x EV/EBIT. |
| **EV/Revenue** | **DISCARD** | Blind to profitability. USL earns an ~18% EBITDA margin vs lower-margin peers; a sales multiple penalises exactly the mix-led margin expansion that defines the investment case. |

**DCF** is retained as the **intrinsic-value anchor / bear-case floor**, not as a co-equal weight, for the reasons in §5.

## 3. Choosing the Statistic: Mean, Median, Quartile?

The workbook computes mean, median, lower- and upper-quartile peer multiples. With a **3-peer set**, the mean is easily skewed, so we anchor the **target EV/EBITDA to the peer median (39.2x FY27E)** and then apply a **modest discount to 38.0x**:

- **Median over mean** — robust to the small, dispersed sample (UB 40.9x, Radico 39.2x, ABDL 31.8x).
- **Slight discount to median (39.2x → 38.0x)** — USL's volume growth is slower than Radico's (premiumisation-led ~9% revenue vs Radico's ~25%), which we offset against USL's superior portfolio, margins and net-cash balance sheet. Net: a touch below the median, a clear premium to mass-peer ABDL.
- Lower- and upper-quartile multiples are used to frame the **bear/bull scenarios** (§6), not the base case.

**Target multiples adopted:** EV/EBITDA **38.0x** FY27E · P/E **52.0x** FY27E (USL-normalised).

## 5. DCF — Read it as a Floor, Not a Fair Value

The model's DCF returns **₹599** (WACC 11.0%, terminal g 6.0%). That is ~52% below the market and should be read as a **bear-case floor**, for two structural reasons:

1. **Working-capital drag.** `Assumptions` hard-codes **97 receivable days and 135 inventory days** (historical medians — whisky maturation + slow state beverage corporations). This absorbs cash (NWC change −₹402 cr in FY27) and depresses FCF far below EBIT.
2. **A 4-year explicit window** pushes **~86% of enterprise value into the terminal value** (PV of TV ₹34,975 cr of EV ₹40,850 cr), making the output hyper-sensitive to the WACC–g spread.

The DCF is therefore informative as a downside marker (it says the current price already discounts years of flawless premiumisation), but it is too conservative to drive the target — hence the 15% weight.

## 7. Recommended Corrections to the Workbook

These materially affect the conclusion and should be actioned:

1. **Mark price to latest close (high priority).** `Comparables!C12` uses Mkt Cap **₹96,196 cr (= ₹1,322/share)**, but the latest close in `Beta Calculation!F5` is **₹1,246.7**. Update Mkt Cap to **~₹90,685 cr**. The stale figure **overstates CMP by ~6%** and understates implied upside across every multiple.
2. **Quarantine the P/E peer comps.** Footnote that United Breweries (beer) and high-growth Radico/ABDL are not P/E-comparable to USL; rely on EV/EBITDA and a USL-normalised P/E. **Add genuine spirits comparables** — Tilaknagar Industries (India pure-play) and, for context, global majors Pernod Ricard / Diageo / Campari — to give the relative set credibility.
3. **Extend the DCF explicit horizon** from 4 to 8–10 years so the premiumisation runway is captured in the explicit period rather than the terminal value (currently ~86% of EV). This is the single biggest reason the DCF prints low.
4. **Document and revisit CAPM inputs.** CAPM = Rf 7% + β 0.92 × ERP 4% ≈ 10.7% (rounded to 11%). An ERP of 4% is low versus India's long-run (~6–7%); cite the source, and use the adjusted beta (0.923) consistently.
5. **Sensitise working capital.** 97 DSO / 135 DIO are historical medians; if USL delivers on guided WC efficiency, FCF and the DCF rise — add a WC sensitivity to the DCF.

## 8. Recommendation & Risks

**HOLD · Target ₹1,240 (−0.5%).** USL is a best-in-class compounder, but the premiumisation story is fairly priced at 34.5x FY27E EV/EBITDA. We see no margin of safety at current levels and would **accumulate below ₹1,100–1,150** (toward the relative bear case) and **trim above ₹1,450** (approaching the bull case). Re-rating toward the bull case (₹1,592) is the key positive catalyst; the DCF floor (₹599) is the key caution.

**Key valuation drivers:** P&A mix-up and pricing (gross margin 46%→47%, `Scenarios`); operating leverage on a fixed cost base; net-cash balance sheet (₹2,727 cr) and ~67% payout; Godawan/luxury and tequila (Don Julio) optionality.

**Key risks:** (i) state excise/route-to-market disruption and pricing lag vs input inflation; (ii) the working-capital intensity that caps cash conversion; (iii) a sector P/E de-rating that would pull peer-relative value down; (iv) premium-segment competition from Pernod Ricard, Radico and Allied Blenders.
